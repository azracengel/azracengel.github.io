# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

This website is a personal portfolio for the owner to present their background, experiences, interests, and contact information to classmates, professors, recruiters, and other visitors. The primary purpose is to help people quickly understand who the owner is, what they have done, and how to get in touch.

The site should feel professional, modern, and personal. It should be visually polished and easy to navigate, inspired by the clean, minimal, high-quality aesthetic of Apple while also incorporating the clarity and approachable structure seen in Airbnb.

## 2. Product Goal

Create a personal website that:
- Introduces the owner clearly and professionally
- Highlights relevant education, experiences, and projects
- Shows personal interests and values
- Makes it easy for recruiters and professors to evaluate fit and reach out
- Provides a simple, memorable user experience across desktop and mobile

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary audience
- Recruiters
- Professors

### Secondary audience
- Classmates
- Friends
- General visitors interested in learning more about the owner

## 4. User Needs

### Recruiters and professors
- Quickly understand who the owner is
- See educational and professional background
- Review relevant experience and projects
- Identify ways to contact the owner

### Classmates and general visitors
- Learn more about the owner’s personality, interests, and experiences
- Navigate easily between sections of the site
- Feel that the site is welcoming and human-centered

## 5. Core User Experience Goals

- Visitors should understand the owner’s identity within seconds of arriving
- The homepage should immediately communicate value and credibility
- The content should be easy to scan rather than overwhelming
- Navigation should be intuitive and consistent
- The site should feel polished without being crowded or cluttered

## 6. Non-Goals

This project does not include:
- A complex blog or news platform
- A contact form requiring backend infrastructure
- User accounts or authentication
- A large portfolio CMS or database
- Social media feed integration
- Heavy animation or distracting visual effects

## 7. Information Architecture

The site will use a simple top navigation with five sections:
1. Home
2. About Me
3. Experience
4. Interests
5. Contact

The navigation should be straightforward and consistent across screen sizes.

## 8. Page Requirements

### 8.1 Home

The Home page should serve as the primary introduction and first impression.

Requirements:
- Display the owner’s name prominently
- Include a short, professional introduction or positioning statement
- Highlight key experience or achievements
- Showcase selected projects or notable work
- Include a clear call to action that directs visitors to learn more or contact the owner
- Keep the layout clean, modern, and visually focused

Content priority:
- Name
- Quick identity statement
- Key experience highlights
- Project links
- Contact or “Learn more” entry point

### 8.2 About Me

This section should explain who the owner is beyond a résumé.

Requirements:
- Personal biography
- Education/background summary
- Personal values, motivations, or interests
- A human-centered, approachable tone

Content priority:
- Who the owner is
- What they care about
- What makes them unique as a student, professional, or person

### 8.3 Experience

This section should summarize the owner’s professional, academic, and project-based experience.

Requirements:
- Timeline or organized list of experiences
- Education history if relevant
- Work, internship, research, or leadership experience
- Key projects or accomplishments
- Clear, readable presentation of responsibilities and outcomes

Content priority:
- Relevance to future opportunities
- Strong and credible achievements
- Clear progression over time

### 8.4 Interests

This section should show personal interests and character.

Requirements:
- Display interests in a clean and engaging way
- Include academic, professional, or personal interests
- Highlight hobbies, passions, or areas of curiosity
- Keep the tone authentic and approachable

Content priority:
- What the owner enjoys investigating or building
- What makes the site feel personal and complete

### 8.5 Contact

This section should make it easy for visitors to get in touch.

Requirements:
- Include email address
- Include LinkedIn profile
- Include GitHub profile
- Use clear labels and strong visibility
- Keep the layout simple and professional

Preferred contact setup:
- Email
- LinkedIn
- GitHub

## 9. Content Strategy

The site should balance professional clarity with personal warmth.

The content strategy should prioritize:
- Concise, readable copy
- Strong written summaries rather than long paragraphs
- Clear hierarchy of information
- Consistent tone across sections
- Honest, polished representation of the owner’s background

The owner may later update:
- Experience and projects as new opportunities arise
- Personal photo if one is added
- Resume-like information as it changes over time

## 10. Visual Design Requirements

### Design direction
- Clean and modern
- Minimal but high-quality
- Spacious layout with generous white space
- Strong visual focus on typography and imagery
- Professional presentation without feeling sterile

### Inspiration
- Apple: clean visual design, modern presentation, minimal clutter
- Airbnb: approachable aesthetics, clear organization, easy navigation

### Design principles
- Keep the page easy to scan
- Avoid overwhelming the visitor with too much information at once
- Use a polished, consistent visual system
- Prioritize readability and clear section separation
- Ensure the site feels personal without becoming cluttered

## 11. Functional Requirements

- The site must have clear section-based navigation
- The design must work properly on desktop and mobile screens
- The homepage must clearly introduce the owner and feature selected experience/project highlights
- Each section must be easy to navigate and clearly labeled
- The Contact section must include direct links to the owner’s email, LinkedIn, and GitHub
- Content should be easy to update as the owner gains new experience

## 12. Non-Functional Requirements

- Fast page load and simple deployment
- Mobile-responsive layout
- Good readability across common browser sizes
- Low visual complexity for a premium feel
- Maintainable structure for future updates

## 13. User Stories

- As a recruiter, I want to quickly understand the owner’s background so I can assess interest and fit.
- As a professor, I want to see the owner’s academic and project experience so I can understand their work and interests.
- As a classmate, I want to learn more about the owner’s personality and interests in a welcoming format.
- As a visitor, I want a clear site structure so I can move between sections without confusion.
- As the owner, I want to update my experience and projects over time without redesigning the whole site.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

The website is considered successful when all of the following are true:

1. Visitors can easily find the five main sections: Home, About Me, Experience, Interests, and Contact.
2. The homepage introduces the owner clearly and highlights relevant experience or project work.
3. The site feels modern, clean, and not overwhelming.
4. The site functions correctly on both desktop and mobile devices.
5. Contact information is clearly visible and includes email, LinkedIn, and GitHub.
6. The content is organized in a way that supports recruitment and academic interest while remaining personal and welcoming.
7. The owner can update experiences and information without major restructuring.

## 15. Summary

This personal website should present the owner as a polished, approachable, and professional person with strong academic and/or project experience. The site should prioritize quick understanding, clear navigation, and credibility for recruiters and professors while still feeling warm and personal for classmates and other visitors.
